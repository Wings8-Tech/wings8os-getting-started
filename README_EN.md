## 🛠️ Boot Assistance (Emergency Procedure)

If the automatic boot does not trigger on a specific hardware configuration:

1.  Use the **physical buttons** integrated into your Wings8 Device.
2.  Select your **computer brand** (Dell, HP, Lenovo, etc.).
3.  **Restart**. This action forces the technological alignment with your motherboard.
